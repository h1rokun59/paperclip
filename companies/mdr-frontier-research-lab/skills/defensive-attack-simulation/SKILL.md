---
name: defensive-attack-simulation
description: Model plausible attack paths for defensive reasoning without producing operational abuse instructions.
---

Use this skill to understand what could have happened and what an MDR team should look for.

Rules:

- Keep scenarios defensive and non-operational.
- Do not provide exploit code, credential theft instructions, malware logic, or step-by-step abuse instructions.
- Describe prerequisites, observable events, blast-radius factors, and confidence.
- Tie every scenario to evidence or clearly label it as a hypothesis.

Output scenario cards with prerequisites, attacker objective, defensive observations, confidence, and detection implications.
