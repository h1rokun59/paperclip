---
name: evidence-led-threat-research
description: Build evidence packs from advisories, vendor notes, public reporting, and observable threat facts.
---

Use this skill to ground a case in evidence before the lab simulates or recommends action.

Process:

1. Prefer primary sources such as vendor advisories, security advisories, repository notices, package registry metadata, and official incident writeups.
2. Record publication dates, observed event dates, affected versions, impacted components, and remediation status.
3. Assign confidence levels and explain why.
4. Identify source disagreements or gaps.
5. Map the facts to customer-relevant exposure points.

Output an evidence pack with source list, timeline, fact table, confidence notes, and research gaps.
