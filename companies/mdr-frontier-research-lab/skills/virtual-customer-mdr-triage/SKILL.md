---
name: virtual-customer-mdr-triage
description: Frame simulated MDR customer questions into clear research contracts and decision criteria.
---

Use this skill when a virtual customer asks about a threat, incident, vulnerability, campaign, or supply-chain event.

Process:

1. Identify the customer decision: what they need to decide or do.
2. Separate facts, assumptions, hypotheses, and unknowns.
3. Define the affected assets, software, identities, environments, and time window.
4. Decide what specialist work is needed: evidence research, attack simulation, detection design, response planning, or synthesis.
5. Define the expected advisory format and confidence standard.

Output a concise case brief with scope, assumptions, deliverables, and next actions.
