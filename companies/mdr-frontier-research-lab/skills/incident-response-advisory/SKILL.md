---
name: incident-response-advisory
description: Convert MDR research findings into prioritized response guidance for virtual customers.
---

Use this skill to turn research into action.

Process:

1. Group recommendations by urgency: immediate, next 24 hours, next week, and longer-term hardening when useful.
2. Separate confirmed compromise response from exposure-only response.
3. Include containment, eradication, recovery, monitoring, evidence preservation, and communications.
4. Tie each recommendation to a finding, assumption, or risk.
5. Highlight decision owners and escalation points.

Output a customer-facing advisory with prioritized actions and rationale.
