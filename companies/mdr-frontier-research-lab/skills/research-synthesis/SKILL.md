---
name: research-synthesis
description: Reconcile specialist findings into clear MDR conclusions, recommendations, and residual risk.
---

Use this skill when multiple research threads need to become one advisory.

Process:

1. Compare findings from evidence research, simulation, detection, and response planning.
2. Resolve contradictions by checking source quality and assumptions.
3. Mark each conclusion as fact, likely inference, plausible hypothesis, or unresolved.
4. Preserve specialist caveats that affect customer decisions.
5. Write a concise executive answer followed by technical detail.

Output a final advisory that is practical, defensible, and honest about uncertainty.
