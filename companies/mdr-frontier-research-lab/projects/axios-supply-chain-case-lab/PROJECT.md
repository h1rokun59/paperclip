This project runs the first MDR research case for MDR Frontier Research Lab.

The virtual customer asks: "We depend on Axios and want to understand what attacks could have been possible during the supply-chain compromise, how we could detect exposure or misuse, and what response actions are most appropriate."

The expected output is a customer-ready MDR advisory that covers attack feasibility, detection strategy, and response recommendations.
